#Junior Jobs
Welcome to my first application!
This is an application for people looking for junior, graduate, an apprenticeship roles in the UK.

The idea is to solve the problem of having to search through all of the offers that LinkedIn or Indeed provide. It is meant to give you the offers that you're more likely to be hired for, or that fit your goal the best.

Eventually, these are the key features that the application is meant to have:

    -The main page with all the roles from different hiring websites that you can apply for.

    -A feature to add your Skills/Degree to filter the offers.

    -Filters so you can search by wage, location, etc...

    -A "Hiring likeliness" meter, that gives you a score from 1-100 that tells you how fit you are for the role.

    -For the people who are interested, it will also show if the company has recently provided any work visas.

    -In the future I would love this to also have a feature so you can get advise on which roles you should be looking for based on your Skills.

It's not like I'm one to need a work visa, but I do feel like there's a lot of interest now to get a job that provide's it, but no tools.
